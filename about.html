About LootRadar

A quality-first view of PC game prices

LootRadar is built for players who want a good game, not merely the largest percentage badge on the page. It narrows a busy price feed into a more useful question: which current listings pair a worthwhile price with credible signs that the game is worth playing?

What LootRadar does

CheapShark supplies the store and price listings. LootRadar normalizes those listings, removes obvious add-ons and low-confidence entries, then ranks the remaining deals using game quality, price value, discount strength, review confidence, and player interest.

The result is a selective starting point, not another wall of discount labels. You can still change the filters, search the full qualifying snapshot, and inspect the score behind any listing.

How current are the prices?

The published snapshot refreshes every three hours. A retailer can change a price, edition, region restriction, or availability between refreshes, so the retailer’s checkout page is always the final word.

Why the filters are strict

Cheap does not automatically mean worthwhile. The default view favors complete games with credible player feedback and filters out obvious DLC, soundtracks, virtual currency, cosmetic packs, demos, and other listings that are not full games.

A huge discount also cannot carry a weak game by itself. Review quality, review volume, and price context all contribute to the LootRadar Deal Score.

What LootRadar does not promise

  • Not every PC retailer is covered. LootRadar works with the participating stores returned by CheapShark.
  • Not every game has been personally tested. Rankings use available price, review, and metadata signals rather than a claim of hands-on review.
  • A Deal Score is not an objective verdict. It is a transparent way to sort the current snapshot.
  • A listed price is not automatically an all-time low. Historical context appears only when CheapShark returns it for an opened listing.

Accounts, privacy, and saved preferences

You do not need an account to browse. Watchlist targets and some preferences can stay on the device you are using. Optional sign-in can sync recommendation preferences through Supabase. LootRadar also uses aggregate analytics and may display advertising; the privacy policy explains those services and the choices available to you.

How the site is funded

LootRadar is free to use. It may earn revenue from advertising or eligible affiliate links. Those relationships do not change prices, Deal Scores, collection eligibility, or ranking order.

Questions and corrections

If a listing looks wrong, a page is unclear, or you have a useful feature suggestion, email contact@thelootradar.com. Good corrections are more valuable than polite silence.
