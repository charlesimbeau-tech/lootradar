The five-deal shortlist

5 PC game deals worth buying this week

Published July 29, 2026 · Prices checked July 29, 2026 at 10:32 a.m. EDT

There are hundreds of discounted games in the latest LootRadar snapshot and most of them do not deserve a second of your time. These five clawed their way to the top because the price is backed by an enormous pile of positive player reviews, rather than by a percentage someone made large on purpose.

Treat it as a starting point, not a shopping list. Take the one that matches what you actually feel like playing, check the edition and activation details, and walk away from the other four with a completely clear conscience.

Read this bit first

How these five got here

All five cleared LootRadar's usual content and quality checks, then finished near the top of the current snapshot on Deal Score. That score weighs player and critic signals, how strong the current price is, how deep the cut goes, how much the reviews prove, and how many people care. Review volume breaks the close ties, so a handful of glowing early ratings cannot impersonate a consensus.

What no score can tell you is whether a genre suits you or when you will actually find the evening. That part is still your job. The LootRadar methodology has the full weighting and the honest limits.
