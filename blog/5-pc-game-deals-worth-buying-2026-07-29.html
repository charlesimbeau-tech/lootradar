The five-deal shortlist

5 PC game deals worth buying this week

Published July 29, 2026 · Prices checked July 29, 2026 at 10:32 a.m. EDT

There are hundreds of discounted games in the latest LootRadar snapshot. Most do not need your attention. These five rose to the top because the current price is backed by a large body of positive player reviews, not just an oversized percentage badge.

This is a starting point, not a five-game shopping list. Pick the game that suits what you actually want to play, confirm the edition and activation details, and leave the rest without remorse.

Disco Elysium - The Final Cut

$8.88 $39.99 at IndieGala

At 92% positive across roughly 57,100 Steam reviews, Disco Elysium has unusually deep evidence behind its reputation. The 78% price cut makes this a strong entry point if you want a dialogue-heavy detective RPG and do not mind trading conventional combat for choices, failure, and a lot of reading.

Red Dead Redemption 2

$13.20 $59.99 at Green Man Gaming

A 92% positive rating from more than 307,000 Steam reviews gives this open-world western one of the strongest confidence signals in the snapshot. The pace is deliberate and the install is substantial, so buy it for the long campaign and richly observed world, not because you need something quick for the weekend.

The Ultimate edition combines a low single-digit price with an 88% positive rating from about 87,500 reviews. It is the most straightforward value here for fighting-game players, but check that the included roster and online population match what you expect before choosing it over a newer entry.

Slay the Spire

$6.24 $24.99 on Steam

Slay the Spire is the critical-consensus pick: 97% positive from roughly 76,400 reviews. Runs are compact, but learning how cards, relics, and enemy patterns fit together can last for hundreds of hours. At 75% off, it is easy to recommend to anyone curious about deck-building games.

How the five were chosen

Each game passed LootRadar's normal content and quality checks, then ranked near the top of the current snapshot by Deal Score. That score weighs player and critic signals, the strength of the current price, discount depth, review confidence, and player interest.
