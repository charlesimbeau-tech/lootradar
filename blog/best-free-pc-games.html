Free PC games worth knowing about

Reviewed July 2026 · LootRadar guide

“Free” can mean a permanent game, a limited-time giveaway, or a store built around optional purchases. Those are different bargains. This guide separates them and highlights games that offer a substantial experience without an upfront price.

Monetization, account requirements, and availability can change. Check the official store page before installing, and decide what kinds of purchases or progression systems you are comfortable having in the room.

Free-to-play shooters

  • Counter-Strike 2 — A demanding tactical shooter built around precise movement, map knowledge, and team economy. The competitive ceiling is the feature.
  • Valorant — Tactical rounds meet character abilities. It rewards coordinated teams and careful utility use as much as clean aim.
  • Apex Legends — A battle royale distinguished by fluid movement, readable squad roles, and gunplay that remains satisfying outside the final circle.
  • The Finals — Objective play unfolds inside destructible arenas. The chaos is useful: changing the building can be as important as winning the firefight.

Competitive games change quickly. Before choosing one, check its current player population, region support, anti-cheat requirements, and store description of paid items.

Free-to-play action games and RPGs

  • Path of Exile — A dense action RPG for players who want build experimentation, complicated systems, and a long endgame more than a gentle introduction.
  • Warframe — Fast cooperative combat wrapped around years of missions, equipment, and progression. It offers breadth, though new players should expect to learn its vocabulary.
  • Genshin Impact — A polished open-world action RPG with strong exploration and character design. Its randomized character acquisition deserves a deliberate spending limit.

A free entry price does not make time or convenience free. Check which progression items are sold, how often the game asks you to return, and whether the core play remains satisfying without paying.

Choose the game for its play, then inspect the business model.

Strategy, MOBAs, and card games

  • Dota 2 — A systems-heavy competitive game where drafting, positioning, timing, and item decisions create an intimidating but unusually deep team contest.
  • League of Legends — Faster to read than Dota but hardly simple, with a large character roster and a strong emphasis on lane control and team fights.
  • Marvel Snap — A compact card game built around short matches, simultaneous turns, and the decision to raise or retreat from the stakes.

Card and character games can tie variety to collection progress. Review the current acquisition system before deciding whether “free” gives you enough room to play the way you want.

MMOs with free access

  • Guild Wars 2 — The core experience provides broad exploration and public events, while expansions and account upgrades sit outside the free tier.
  • Final Fantasy XIV Free Trial — A large trial for players interested in a story-led MMO. Social and account restrictions apply, so read the current trial terms first.
  • Star Wars: The Old Republic — Strong class stories make the free portion appealing as a solo RPG, though convenience and later content are shaped by account status.

Limited-time giveaways

Epic Games Store, Steam, GOG, publishers, and other retailers sometimes offer paid games for a zero-dollar claim. These are not evergreen recommendations: the title and window can change before an article catches up.

Always confirm three things on the official page: whether you keep the game, when the claim window ends, and which account or launcher receives the license. Prime Gaming offers should also be described accurately as benefits included with a paid Prime membership.

Build a library you might actually use

  1. Claim limited-time games because you want them, not because the button is temporarily exciting.
  2. Keep a short list of free-to-play games instead of installing six competing daily routines.
  3. Set spending limits before entering a store designed around optional purchases.
  4. Review account, launcher, region, and ownership terms on the official page.
  5. Remove what you do not play. Storage space is allowed to have standards.

Free is a price. Worthwhile is still the test.
