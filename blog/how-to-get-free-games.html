How to get free PC games legally

Reviewed July 2026 · LootRadar guide

“Free” describes several different arrangements: a game you claim and keep, a title supported by optional purchases, a temporary trial, or access included with a paid membership. The price may be zero in each case, but the ownership and obligations are not.

Use official storefronts, publisher pages, and established communities to find offers. Before installing anything, confirm who is distributing it, what account or launcher it requires, and whether access ends with the promotion.

Epic Games Store giveaways

Epic Games Store regularly features limited-time games that can be added to an account without an upfront payment. The available title, claim window, region, and terms can change, so check the store’s own free-games section for the current offer.

Claiming during the promotional window usually adds the game to your library, but read the listing before assuming permanent ownership. A free weekend or trial is a different offer, even when the same zero-dollar button is involved.

Amazon Prime Gaming

Prime Gaming offers games and in-game items as a benefit of an active Amazon Prime membership. That makes the individual claim an included benefit, not a free service in its own right.

Delivery varies. A game may use the Amazon Games app or provide a key for another storefront. Check the activation deadline and whether a claimed game remains available after the membership ends. The offer page should answer both questions before you add another launcher to your desktop.

Steam promotions

Steam distinguishes among permanently free games, limited-time “free to keep” promotions, demos, and free weekends. Read the banner carefully. “Play for free” may mean access ends on Monday; “add to account” may indicate a lasting license.

Publisher giveaways can be brief and irregular. Use the Steam store page as the source of truth, and be cautious with social posts that omit the end time or link through an unfamiliar domain.

GOG giveaways

GOG sometimes offers limited-time claims during store promotions. GOG is known for DRM-free installers, but individual terms still matter. Confirm that the offer adds the full game to your library and note the claim deadline.

Some classic games are permanently free rather than promotional. Those are useful precisely because no countdown is involved.

A zero-dollar price is the beginning of the check, not the end of it.

itch.io and developer releases

itch.io hosts commercial releases, free downloads, pay-what-you-want projects, demos, prototypes, and game-jam entries. That range is the appeal. It also means you should read each project page rather than treating the storefront as one uniform catalog.

Developer and publisher sites may distribute free games, open betas, or older releases directly. Use the official domain or a verified store page. A download mirror posted by a stranger is not improved by an enthusiastic thumbnail.

Publisher promotions and open betas

Studios sometimes run promotions around anniversaries, updates, or new releases. Open betas can also provide temporary access to an unfinished game. Neither should be confused with owning the finished product.

  • Giveaway: confirm the claim deadline and whether the license remains in your account.
  • Free weekend: expect access to end; progress may carry into a purchase, but verify that on the store page.
  • Open beta: expect incomplete software, resets, and limited availability.
  • Demo: expect a deliberately limited portion of the game.

Free-to-play games

Free-to-play games charge no entry price but may sell cosmetics, characters, inventory space, convenience, or randomized items. The relevant question is not simply whether spending is optional. It is whether the free experience remains enjoyable without constant pressure to spend.

Before committing time, check how progression works, what purchases affect play, whether parental controls are appropriate, and whether the game requires a separate account. Our guide to free PC games worth knowing about separates substantial free experiences from time-limited claims.

Subscriptions are paid access

Game Pass, Humble Choice, Prime Gaming, and other memberships may include games, but the membership has a price. Catalog access can end when a subscription lapses or when a title leaves the service. Some benefits provide a permanent key; others provide access only while the agreement remains active.

Describe the arrangement accurately before deciding whether it is good value. “Included with a service I already buy” can be a sensible deal. It still is not the same as free.

A quick legitimacy check

  1. Open the official store, publisher, or developer page.
  2. Confirm the exact game, edition, region, and activation platform.
  3. Read whether the offer is permanent, time-limited, or tied to a membership.
  4. Check the claim deadline and any activation deadline.
  5. Decline downloads that require disabling security tools or using an unknown installer.
  6. Remember that a free game can still cost attention, storage, and time.
