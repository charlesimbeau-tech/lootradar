Excellent indie games that often cost less than $5

Reviewed July 2026 · LootRadar guide

Cheap does not mean small. Every game below is a proper, memorable, argue-about-it-with-friends game that happens to hit around five dollars when the stores are feeling generous. Several of them are better than things that cost twelve times as much.

Standard warning: none of these prices are promised. They move with the date, the region, the store, and the edition, and a few of these spend most of the year comfortably above this range. Treat this as a watchlist to point at the next deep sale, not a menu.

Runs built on beautiful, agonizing choices

Vampire Survivors

You move. That is the whole control scheme. Twenty minutes later the screen is an unreadable fireworks display of your own making and you are somehow still alive. It costs almost nothing and it has eaten more evenings than games with a hundred times the budget.

Slay the Spire

The deckbuilder that made everyone else start building decks. Every card you add changes the odds of drawing every other card, which means the strongest card in the shop is frequently a trap. Four characters, four completely different ways to think. You will say “one more run” and you will be lying.

Into the Breach

The enemies tell you exactly what they are about to do, and it is still brutally hard. Every turn is a small perfect puzzle about shoving a giant bug into the path of its friend and choosing which building you are willing to lose. From the FTL people, and just as sharp.

FTL: Faster Than Light

Fuel, repairs, crew, and a jump map that is always slightly worse than you hoped. What makes the losses stick is that the game always shows its working. You never die to bad luck alone. You die to that decision, three jumps back, that you knew was greedy.

Stick them on a watchlist and let the price tell you when it is time.

Platformers with something to say

Celeste

Ferociously demanding movement paired with checkpoints so generous you never lose more than four seconds. It respects your time and refuses to lower the bar, which almost nothing else manages. And the story about the mountain turns out to be a story about something else entirely.

Hollow Knight

An enormous, sad, gorgeous bug kingdom that explains almost nothing and expects you to keep up. Getting lost is the content. Forty hours in you will still be finding rooms, and it is worth many times its usual sale price.

Katana ZERO

Every room is a one-hit-kill puzzle you solve at full speed, restarting instantly until the whole thing plays back like a choreographed action sequence. Then the story starts pulling threads you did not notice it had.

Stories that could only be games

Undertale

A small RPG that is paying very close attention to how you play it. Whether you fight, whether you spare, whether you reload. Go in knowing as little as possible, and please do not look up the endings first.

To the Moon

Two doctors walk backwards through a dying man’s memories to build him the life he wanted. Mechanically it is barely a game. It will still take you apart, and the soundtrack does half the damage.

Oxenfree

Conversations overlap, characters talk over each other, and the story keeps moving whether or not you say anything. It feels less like picking dialogue options and more like being in a group of friends where the ghost stuff has started happening.

A Short Hike

You climb a mountain. You chat with some birds. You get gloriously sidetracked. It is about ninety minutes long and it is exactly as long as it should be. The perfect thing for an evening when you have nothing left in the tank.

Systems you will still be thinking about in bed

Terraria

Building, digging, crafting, and a boss ladder that never quite ends, with no correct way to play any of it. A four-dollar sale price is hiding an entire hobby. People have thousands of hours in this. Consider yourself warned.

Papers, Please

Checking paperwork at a border crossing turns out to be one of the most stressful things a game has ever asked you to do. Rules pile up, your family needs medicine, and every act of kindness costs you something real. Glory to Arstotzka.

Mini Metro

Draw subway lines for a city that will not stop growing. Your elegant little diagram becomes a screaming capacity crisis around minute twelve, and because the whole thing is so clean, you can see precisely which lazy decision doomed it.

Baba Is You

The rules of the level are words sitting on the floor, and you can push them. Shove “wall is stop” apart and walls stop stopping. It is the smartest puzzle game in years and it will make you feel simultaneously brilliant and extremely stupid.

Short, dark, and unforgettable

Limbo

Black and white, no dialogue, and a series of physical puzzles punctuated by deaths you did not see coming. It is short on purpose. Any longer and the atmosphere would thin out.

Inside

Playdead did it again, bigger and much stranger. The animation alone is worth the price. Go in blind, tell no one what happens, and expect to sit quietly for a minute afterwards.

How to actually shop this list

Check the price now instead of trusting the sale you remember from last winter. Compare the exact edition, note which launcher the key activates on, and check whether that tempting bundle actually includes the base game. For the older picks, skim recent reviews, because a game with a great 2014 reputation can have a rough relationship with a 2026 GPU.
