# LootRadar YouTube Community Poll Design

**Date:** July 29, 2026
**Status:** Approved for publishing

## Objective

Create a low-friction YouTube Community poll that encourages PC players to share the factor they value most when judging a game deal.

## Post Format

- YouTube Community poll
- No introductory caption
- No website link
- No hashtags
- No image

## Poll Question

What matters most when you judge a PC game deal?

## Poll Options

1. Game quality and reviews
2. Current price and value
3. Discount percentage
4. Historical-low pricing

## Publishing Requirements

- Publish from the LootRadar YouTube channel.
- Preserve the wording and option order exactly.
- Do not mark any response as objectively correct.
- Publish immediately rather than scheduling.

## Verification

After publishing, confirm that the post is visible in YouTube Studio or on the channel, contains exactly four poll options, and includes no caption text.
