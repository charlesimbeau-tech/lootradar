How these deals qualify

To appear here a listing must come from the Steam storefront, cost $10 or less, and pass the default eligibility rules. Whatever qualifies is ranked by Deal Score, which weighs player and critic signals, how strong the price is, how deep the cut goes, review volume, and player interest. DLC, soundtracks, demos, and currency packs are not invited.
