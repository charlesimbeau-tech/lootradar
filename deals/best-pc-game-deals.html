It is a starting point, not an instruction. Genre, mood, and how much time you actually have still matter more than any number we can calculate. Every card tells you the store, the current price, how solid the review evidence is, the Deal Score, and the specific reason this offer climbed.

How these deals qualify

A listing has to clear the default eligibility rules and score at least 75, the site's "Great deal" boundary. That score mixes game quality, price value, discount strength, review confidence, and player interest. Add-ons, soundtracks, demos, currency packs, and anything with thin evidence get filtered out before ranking even starts. What remains is ordered by Deal Score, with review volume breaking the ties.

We do the filtering and the scoring. The store decides what you actually pay and whether it is still in stock. Listings can change after the snapshot, or in the time it takes you to click through.
